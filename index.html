Case analysis & context

9 August 2026 · Verified press coverage

The presidential endorsement

On Singapore’s 61st National Day, former President Halimah Yacob wore Batik Sayang’s signature Vanda Miss Joaquim Big Brooch — a piece she personally purchased herself — to the Padang parade grounds.

Moments later, Member of Parliament Nadia Ahmad Samdin was photographed wearing Batik Sayang floral earrings. Serena Wong had also gifted a bespoke small kerongsang to Mdm Halimah, and a brooch to Loo Tze Lui, wife of Prime Minister Lawrence Wong.

One night, a brand changed

Serena Wong, 33, a former music teacher, founded Batik Sayang in April 2024. Every piece is handmade — 1–5 hours of wax, dye and hand-finishing per piece.

After the parade, she opened limited pre-orders of around 50 pieces across the collection, and spent hours replying to DMs and enquiries the day after NDP. The demand arrived overnight. The system to catch it did not exist yet.

Operational friction diagnostic

Virality withnowhere to land.

When the state broadcast aired and press outlets profiled Batik Sayang, the enquiries flooded into one place: Instagram DMs at @batik_sayangsg.

There was no website, no product catalogue, and no checkout. Every question about price, size, availability and delivery had to be answered by hand — by the same hands that make every piece.

The remedy: turn the accidental DM frenzy into an organised, high-fashion editorial engine with an instant path from “I love this” to a structured, prepaid pre-order.

That is what this proposal builds: five editorial product shots, a seven-day content calendar, and a DM-to-order shop concept — all in Batik Sayang’s own visual language.
