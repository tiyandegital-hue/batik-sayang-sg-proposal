Transparent principles & FAQ

Engineered for Serena’s real workflow

Why are prices marked [PLACEHOLDER]?

Tiyan Digital does not invent quotes. Per-piece prices are not publicly listed anywhere, and the right retainer depends on Serena’s real capacity — so the scope is fixed first, and the price is agreed with her, not guessed for her.

What payment methods are supported?

Direct PayNow or FAST bank transfer. No merchant platform fees, so every dollar of a drop reaches the studio.

Can we start with the one-off shoot and upgrade?

Yes. Starting with the one-off shoot is the recommended first step. Whether any of that investment is credited toward a retainer is agreed with Serena in the signed Scope of Work — it is not assumed here.

What happens to the AI samples?

The five direction samples stay labelled as AI-generated. Once Serena approves the direction, her real pieces are shot for real — the samples are a moodboard, never a substitute for her craft.
