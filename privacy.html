Article 01 · Singapore statutory standard

Scope & purpose of this proposal site

This website serves as an interactive digital commerce and content-engine proposal prepared exclusively by Tiyan Digital for the benefit and review of Batik Sayang, founded by artisan Serena Wong in Singapore.

All interactive components — including the lookbook gallery, content calendar and e-commerce checkout simulations — are demonstrations of a proposed service. No purchase can be completed on this site, and no real customer order is processed here.

Compliance benchmarks adhere to the statutory tenets of the Singapore Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) under the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

Article 02 · Consent framework

Collection of personal data

Under Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA), personal data refers to any data, whether true or not, about an individual who can be identified from that data. This site collects only what is voluntarily provided:

  • Contact names — voluntarily provided by prospective atelier collaborators through the intake form.
  • Instagram handles — @handles submitted for bespoke social curation reviews.
  • Messages — the text of an enquiry, provided voluntarily.

Excluded & protected data. We do not capture, store or process payment card details directly. Shop flows on this site use mock sandbox parameters only, and genuine settlements — if the engagement proceeds — route exclusively through peer-to-peer Singapore PayNow QR banking protocols, outside this site.

Demonstration form. The intake form on the Contact page is a demonstration handler: entries are validated in your browser and are not transmitted to, or stored by, any server. To actually contact us, use Instagram DM at @batik_sayangsg.

Article 03 · Strict limitation

Use and disclosure of information

Information received through this proposal environment is stewarded under rigorous purpose limitation, and used only to:

  • Coordinate creative workshop scheduling between Serena Wong and Tiyan Digital creative strategists.
  • Deliver scope clarification and consultation briefs — bespoke audit notes and targeted production estimates for the proposed editorial roll-out.
  • Zero data commercialisation. Submitted records are under no circumstances sold, leased, rented or distributed to advertising data brokers or third parties.

Article 04 · Artisan attribution

Artisan intellectual property & visual transparency

Tiyan Digital operates this proposal under complete intellectual reverence for Southeast Asian traditional craft.

Exclusive ownership of Batik Sayang

All handmade batik accessories, wax-resist patterns, fabric treatments, brand insignias and Singapore Vanda Miss Joaquim motifs depicted herein remain the exclusive, inalienable intellectual property of Serena Wong.

Synthetic & generative simulation disclosure

Visual previews and model renders presented in the photoshoot pitch gallery are simulated editorial direction samples generated with AI tools. Every such image carries a visible “AI-GENERATED SAMPLE” badge and a transparency note. They are never presented as finished physical products, and real photography of Serena Wong’s pieces replaces them once the direction is approved.

Article 05 · Infrastructure safeguards

Data retention & protection

To prevent unauthorised access, collection, use or disclosure, Tiyan Digital implements strict administrative and technical safeguards, including:

  • SSL / TLS encryption — end-to-end encryption for all data transmitted through forms.
  • Singapore data hosting — servers hosted within sovereign Singapore geographic clusters meeting IMDA / PDPC criteria.
  • Automated purge — records are erased after the pitch evaluation concludes, unless you ask us to keep them or a signed agreement requires retention. No fixed retention period is promised here; the exact period is set in the signed Scope of Work.

Article 06 · Designated officer

Contact the Data Protection Officer (DPO)

In accordance with Sections 11 and 12 of the Personal Data Protection Act, Tiyan Digital has appointed a Data Protection Officer for this proposal.

Direct inquiry channel: DM @batik_sayangsg on Instagram, or use the Contact page and mark your message “PDPA Inquiry — Batik Sayang Proposal”. A dedicated email address for the DPO is provided in the signed Scope of Work once the engagement begins — no email address is published on this proposal site.

All statutory access inquiries receive a formal acknowledgment promptly, and are handled within the timeframes set by the PDPA.
