Terms & Conditions of Proposal

Version 1.0 — Singapore commercial law compliance · Tiyan Digital × Batik Sayang. Crafted to protect the artisan first, and the engagement second.

Document stance — artisan-first protocol: built deliberately around Serena Wong’s manual 1–5 hour wax-resist craftsmanship rhythm. Growth without aggressive funnels. No automatic roll-overs. No locked proprietary accounts. Every Instagram asset, raw file and plan is handed over.

Clause 01.0 · Framework · Non-binding architectural scope

Nature of this proposal

1.1. This proposal document represents an architectural advisory blueprint and commercial scope exploration prepared by Tiyan Digital for Batik Sayang (Serena Wong). It is not, by itself, a binding contract.

1.2. Any financial figures, retainers and production schedules explicitly designated with the label [PLACEHOLDER — TO CONFIRM] are not quoted prices. They are scope markers to be finalised with Serena before any commitment.

1.3. Binding commercial obligations commence only upon the mutual execution of an official Master Services Agreement (MSA) and a countersigned Scope of Work (SOW) articulating finalised deliverables, timelines and payment terms.

Clause 02.0 · Core philosophy · Artisan protection term

Artisan sovereignty & batch limits

2.1. Exclusive production authority. Both parties formally recognise that Batik Sayang’s core brand equity is derived from the laborious, handmade nature of every piece. Production batches may be capped at around 50 units — or any lower volume Serena selects — per capsule drop, and no marketing activity may promise volume beyond that cap.

2.2. Curbing high-pressure automation. Tiyan Digital strictly covenants never to construct, advise or implement synthetic urgency funnels, fake countdowns or false scarcity. Every scarcity claim in the content engine must correspond to a real, physical production limit.

Clause 03.0 · Ethical digital media · Provenance disclosure

AI generation transparency & brand representation

3.1. In the event that synthetic concept models, background generative stylings or algorithmic moodboards are used, conspicuous visual demarcation is mandatory — every AI-generated image carries a visible “AI-GENERATED SAMPLE” badge and a transparency note.

3.2. Digital visuals generated through generative systems must never be passed off as finished physical products. Direction samples are labelled “Studio Digital Concept // Handcrafted Piece In Production” until real photography replaces them.

3.3. Genuine photography capturing Serena Wong’s hands, canting tools, natural indigo dyes and actual pieces is the only photography that may be presented as the product itself.

Clause 04.0 · Heritage rights · Strict IP demarcation

Intellectual property ownership

Batik Sayang / Serena Wong retains:

  • All original wax-drawn motif designs
  • Traditional canting stencils & dye formulas
  • The Batik Sayang trademark and brand identity
  • Personal likeness, studio voice and story

Assigned deliverables transfer:

  • Commissioned Reel scripts & storyboards
  • Bespoke editorial photo colour grades
  • Content calendar architectures
  • IG shop taxonomy & digital guide assets

4.2. All marketing IP formulated by Tiyan Digital specifically for this engagement transfers in full to Batik Sayang upon payment, with no licence-back or reuse without consent.

Clause 05.0 · Commercial clearing · 0% intermediary fee policy

Payment & settlement protocol

5.1. Direct Singapore clearance. All milestone disbursements and monthly retainers are conducted strictly through direct Singapore payment rails — PayNow or FAST bank transfer.

5.2. By utilising native Singapore monetary rails, neither party incurs merchant platform fees on PayNow settlement.

5.3. Invoices are generated electronically. Payment terms, including any credit period, are agreed in the signed Scope of Work — no credit term is fixed by this proposal.

Clause 06.0 · Flexibility · Agility guarantee

Termination & scope adjustment

6.1. Any retainer model chosen is operational on a mutual written-notice period, the length of which is agreed in the signed Scope of Work. Either party may pause or dissolve the active engagement without punitive financial penalties.

6.2. In the event of scope dissolution, Tiyan Digital commits to a friction-free offboarding handover — all assets, accounts, raw files and plans are transferred promptly, on a timetable agreed in the Scope of Work.

6.3. No “hostage assets” or locked logins shall ever occur. Batik Sayang retains full sovereign administration of its Instagram account, customer lists and brand assets at all times.

Clause 07.0 · Jurisdiction · Republic of Singapore

Governing law & dispute resolution

7.1. This proposal, the overarching contractual architecture and all related commercial interactions are governed by the laws of the Republic of Singapore.

7.2. Any dispute or controversy arising out of this collaboration shall first be addressed through good-faith negotiation between the parties, and then through mediation in Singapore, before any formal proceedings.
