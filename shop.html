Phase 3 architecture · Frictionless commerce

The DM-to-order flow:from chaotic inquiries to a 4-step pipeline.

Serena Wong sells exclusively via Instagram DM (@batik_sayangsg) and artisan pop-up markets — with no website and no catalogue. This concept turns that DM thread into a structured, prepaid pre-order flow. It is a concept mock-up, not a live store.

Friction levelZero app installs

Runs directly inside native Instagram direct messages.

Max drop volume~50 units / batch

A hard-stop counter prevents the studio from over-committing.

Settlement gatewayPayNow

Market ground game

Bridging pop-up markets & digital pre-orders

Serena sells in person at artisan pop-up markets as well as online. The shop concept connects the two, so a sold-out table never means a lost customer.

Stand-in QR signage

An elegant wooden table sign carries the drop QR code. When a display piece sells out, visitors scan and lock the next batch on the spot.

Auto-sync ledger

Every IG DM reservation is recorded automatically in Serena’s master sheet — no copying, no double-booking, no lost orders.

Collector VIP list

An opted-in list of Singapore heritage lovers, for early access to the next drop before it opens to the public.
