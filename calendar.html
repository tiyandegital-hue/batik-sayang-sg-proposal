Operational value proposition

Why this content engine protects Serena Wong

Artisanal integrity requires deep quiet. The 7-day calendar replaces chaotic, reactive communication with a planned rhythm.

Fewer repetitive DMs

Answers to the same questions — prices, dimensions, delivery — live in the content itself, so Serena answers each question once, in public.

Caps batches at ~50

Followers are conditioned that each release is capped to what Serena can personally produce. Scarcity is real because the craft is slow.

Anchors premium value

Documenting the 1–5 hour wax and canting journey before drop day lets customers perceive each brooch as the handwork it is.

Protects maker energy

Serena’s week stays at the wooden workbench with the canting pen — not glued to a glowing inbox answering the same message twice.
